# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in our community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

Expected behavior: clean-room technical rigor, constructive feedback, and strict compliance with copyright & anti-circumvention boundaries (no proprietary IPAs, artwork, or FairPlay keys).

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
